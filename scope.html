ThineCraft project scope

TeamCraft

Status: Placeholder project. This document describes intended direction; it does not claim that working functionality exists.

Project website

Purpose

TeamCraft is the planned open communication and meetings platform for teams that want conversations, decisions, and meeting records to remain portable and connected to the work.

Confirmed direction

  • Team and project spaces with channels and durable conversations.
  • Meetings, notes, decisions, follow-up work, and related records kept in context.
  • Connections to ShareCraft sites, DriveCraft files, GanttCraft plans, and FlowCraft processes.
  • Open interfaces, exportable records, self-hosting, and long-term access.
  • Communication treated as organizational knowledge rather than a disposable message stream.

Boundaries and non-goals

  • TeamCraft is not intended to trap conversations in a proprietary archive.
  • It should link communication to durable records without duplicating the primary responsibilities of ShareCraft or DriveCraft.
  • Meeting and messaging features remain conceptual until implementation choices are approved.
  • No implementation, protocol compatibility, release date, or production readiness is currently promised.

Expected integrations

  • ShareCraft spaces and durable organizational records.
  • DriveCraft files and offline continuity.
  • GanttCraft plans and FlowCraft follow-up processes.
  • Identity, presence, notification, search, and governance services shared across ThineCraft.

Open decisions

  • Real-time messaging, federation, calling, meeting, and media architecture.
  • Threading, editing, retention, moderation, export, and legal-hold semantics.
  • Identity, guest participation, presence, and cross-organization collaboration.
  • Notification routing and offline/mobile behavior.

Guidance for AI contributors

  1. Keep communication connected to durable organizational context.
  2. Do not describe planned messaging or meeting capabilities as implemented.
  3. Prefer portable records, open interfaces, exportability, and self-hosting.
  4. Avoid duplicating content ownership that belongs in ShareCraft or DriveCraft.
  5. Record new product decisions in this file before presenting them as settled scope.
